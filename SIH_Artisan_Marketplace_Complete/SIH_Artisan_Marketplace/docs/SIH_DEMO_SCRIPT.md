# SIH Demo Script

## Problem

Many artisans have quality products but face difficulties with digital cataloguing, product discovery, pricing and market access.

## Solution

ArtisanAI converts a product photograph into a structured marketplace catalogue, provides transparent pricing support, and presents the product in a searchable marketplace.

## 2-minute demo

**Step 1 — Upload**
Upload a photograph of an artisan product.

**Step 2 — AI catalogue**
The system identifies visible product characteristics and creates:
- product name
- description
- category
- material estimate
- tags
- search keywords

**Step 3 — Human review**
The artisan or coordinator can edit the generated content before publishing.

**Step 4 — Smart pricing**
The pricing engine combines production cost, labour, craft complexity and target margin to generate a transparent price range.

**Step 5 — Marketplace**
The product appears in a searchable catalogue with image, description, category and price.

## Key innovation

The prototype focuses on reducing the digital effort required from artisans while retaining human approval over AI-generated catalogue information and pricing.
